Support

Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome.

Email

support@stringlings.app

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Open an issue on the project's issue tracker (a free GitHub account is needed).

What helps

  • Your device (for example iPhone SE or iPad) and the app version, shown in the App Store or TestFlight.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

If the problem is with a scanned page, please describe it rather than sending a photo of the music, since most printed music is under copyright.

Stringlings is a one-person, spare-time project, so please be patient with replies.
