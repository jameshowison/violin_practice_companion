Stringlings

Violin practice help at home

Made by a parent, for parents helping their kids through the early stages of learning the violin.

Coming soon for iPhone and iPad.

Photo to fingerings

Take a photo of a page of music. Stringlings reads it and writes the fingering above each note, or shows the fingerings on their own (A1, A3…).

Practice in sections

Reflow a piece into short sections that follow the music's own phrases, and work on one at a time.

Hear it played

Play the whole piece or just a few bars out loud. Each note lights up as it plays.

Your teacher's recording

Record your teacher playing the piece. Stringlings lines the recording up with the music, so you can hear just the section you've chosen.

Numbered notation

Show any piece in jianpu, where the numbers 1 to 7 stand for the notes of the scale.

A tuner

Get the strings in tune before you start.

No sign-ups, no ads, nothing collected

Everything you add stays on your device unless you share it. Read the privacy policy.
